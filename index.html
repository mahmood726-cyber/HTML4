Sensitivity Analysis

Leave-one-out: shows pooled estimate when each study removed. Cumulative: shows how estimate evolved as studies accumulated chronologically. Influence diagnostics identify outliers.

GRADE Assessment

GRADE rates certainty of evidence: High, Moderate, Low, Very Low. RCTs start High; observational starts Low. Downgrade for: risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, publication bias.
